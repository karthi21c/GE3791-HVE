    Paper pattern

      In the exam hall

      • Read the whole paper for 5 minutes. Underline the verb in each question: define, explain, discuss, illustrate.
      • Do the questions you know best first. Keep a clock on the 13-mark answers: about 20 minutes each.
      • For a 13-mark answer: one-line meaning, 3 to 4 points with the Article or law, one real example, a closing line.
      • If you blank out, write the keywords you remember. Marks come for each correct point.
      • Leave 10 minutes at the end to check the question numbers.
